How it works

How Our Academic Writing Service Works — 5 Steps from Brief to Delivery

A good piece of academic writing comes from understanding the brief, choosing the right writer, and reviewing the work properly before it reaches you. Our process is built around those three principles.

The process

Five steps from brief to delivery

1

Share your brief

Send your assignment brief, rubric, deadline, and any reading list or supporting materials through WhatsApp or our intake form. The more detail you share, the more accurately we can quote — and the better the writer can match your programme's expectations.

Send on WhatsApp Use intake form
2

We confirm fit and price

We review the brief and confirm we can support it. You receive a clear quote based on qualification level, page count, and deadline. There are no hidden fees and no last-minute surprises.

Quotes are sent within a few hours during working hours (Sunday–Thursday, 9 am–8 pm UAE time). Outside these hours, a quote is sent the next morning.

3

Matched to a subject expert

Your brief is matched to a writer with relevant subject expertise — MBA, CIPD, engineering, nursing, law, or your specific discipline. Subject fit is the first quality check, not an optional extra.

4

Drafted, then reviewed

The writer drafts the work to your brief and rubric. Before it reaches you, the order passes a quality review against your brief, your rubric, academic English standards, and originality checks.

Brief and rubric check

Verified against your submission requirements before delivery.

Plagiarism check

Copyscape check targeting below 5% similarity on every order.

Academic English review

Grammar, syntax, and citation standards checked throughout.

Owner-led quality audit

At launch, every order is read by the same person who built the service.

5

Delivered to you

You receive polished, properly referenced model answer material in academic English. If something is off, request a revision and we will work through it with you.

Revisions are accepted within seven days of delivery. Most tiers include one to two revisions; dissertation work includes unlimited revisions through supervisor feedback rounds.

Scope

What we support, and what we do not

Honesty about scope is part of how we work.

We support

  • Assignment, coursework, and dissertation writing as model answer material
  • CIPD Level 5 and 7 HR and procurement assignments
  • Editing and proofreading on work you have already written
  • Reference material across business, IT, engineering, nursing, law, and most academic disciplines
  • Research proposals and literature reviews
  • Reflective essays and case studies

We do not support

  • Exams, invigilated assessments, or timed tests
  • Ghostwriting work submitted as the student's own without learning from it
  • Grade guarantees or promised outcomes
  • Work in languages other than English
  • Content that violates your university's academic integrity policy
