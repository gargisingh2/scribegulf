Pricing

Clear, fair pricing based on level and deadline

How pricing works

Three things determine your quote

Qualification level, page count, and deadline. The quote you receive is exact — no hidden fees, no last-minute additions.

Qualification level

College through doctoral — each tier reflects the complexity and expertise required.

Page count

One page equals 250–275 words. Confirmed at quote stage — no ambiguity.

Deadline

Standard delivery is 48–72 hours. Urgent 24-hour delivery is available at roughly twice the standard rate.

Minimum order

AED 120, covering approximately three pages. This covers admin and quality-review time on small briefs.

Our commitment

What pricing does not change

Whatever the tier, four things stay constant on every order.

Subject-matched writer

Your brief always goes to a writer with relevant subject expertise.

Quality review

Every order is reviewed against your brief, rubric, and academic standards before delivery.

Plagiarism and language checks

Copyscape and academic-English checks run before every delivery.

Honest framing

Model answer material, not ghostwriting. No grade guarantees — ever.
