## Product / Service

**What we sell:**
Custom academic model answers and reference material for university students across all qualification levels — from college through to doctoral level. Students share a brief, get matched to a subject-specialist writer, and receive reviewed work ready for use as a reference.

**Primary offer:**
Expert-written model answers and reference papers, delivered in 48-72 hours (standard) or 24 hours (urgent)

**Price point / tiers:**
- College: AED 15/page (standard) | AED 30/page (24-hr)
- Undergraduate: AED 20/page | AED 40/page
- Masters / MBA: AED 35/page | AED 70/page
- CIPD L5 & L7: AED 40/page | AED 80/page
- Dissertation / Thesis: AED 45/page | AED 90/page
- Editing & Proofreading: AED 8/page | AED 16/page

**Key differentiators:**
1. 25 years of education sector expertise behind every order
2. Every order reviewed by a senior editor against the student's rubric before delivery
3. Subject-specialist writers matched to each brief (not a generic pool)
4. Transparent, upfront pricing — no hidden fees
5. Plagiarism-checked before delivery

## Brand Voice and Tone

**Voice descriptors:** Expert, warm, reassuring, direct, credible

**Tone guide:**
- In headlines: Confident and clear — no hedging language. State the benefit plainly.
- In body copy: Conversational but professional — like a helpful, knowledgeable senior who genuinely wants the student to succeed
- In CTAs: Action-oriented, low-pressure — invite rather than demand. "Send us your brief" not "Buy now"
- Never: Overly salesy, gimmicky, slang-heavy, or alarmist

**Example of on-brand copy:**
"You've worked hard to get here. When the writing gets in the way of showing what you know, we step in. Our subject-specialist writers produce model answers that show you exactly how top-level work is structured — so you can learn from it and build your own."

**Example of off-brand copy:**
"Get your essays written fast! Cheap prices! No one will know! Order now and ace your exams!" — too pushy, too transactional, undermines trust
